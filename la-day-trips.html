About the drive times. Every number is a realistic door-to-door estimate from The Q Playa in Playa Vista, assuming you leave at a sane hour. Leaving at 5pm on a Friday? Add a lot. Every stop has a Directions button that opens Google Maps with our address already set as the origin, so you always get the live number.

About the menu links. Every restaurant has two buttons. Menu jumps straight to that exact restaurant's menu — one tap, no digging through a homepage, and it lands on the current menu even after the restaurant redesigns its site. Map opens its Google Maps card for hours, photos and the phone number. Restaurants move their menu pages constantly, so this stays correct where a hardcoded link would quietly rot.

About the Michelin notes. Stars and Bib Gourmands are from the California guide and get reshuffled every year, so treat them as "worth checking" rather than gospel — and book the starred ones weeks out.

Your stamps and photos are private by default. Marking a stop "been there" moves it out of the main list into the Been there tab, where you can set the date you went and add photos. Photos are shrunk to a sensible size and stored in this browser on this device — never uploaded, and they work offline.

Getting photos onto your other devices. Tap a photo and hit Keep in the page. That one saves a compressed copy into the page itself, so it shows up wherever you open this link — and to anyone you share the link with. Everything else stays on the device you added it from. There's a ceiling of 60 kept photos, because the whole page has to stay under 16MB.
