Notes

The drive times assume a weekend. Every number is a door-to-door estimate from The Q Playa on a Saturday or Sunday with normal traffic, not a mileage calculation — and rounded up, because nothing in this city takes the time the map says. Treat them as planning numbers. Every entry has a Directions link that opens Maps with our address already set as the origin, which is where you get the real number for the hour you're actually leaving.

Menus. Each restaurant has a Menu link that goes straight to that exact restaurant's current menu, and a Map link for hours and the phone number. These resolve at the moment you tap them, so they keep working after a restaurant redesigns its site.

Michelin. Stars and Bib Gourmands come from the California guide and get reshuffled every year, so read them as worth checking rather than as fact. Book the starred rooms weeks ahead.

Stamps and photos. Marking a place visited moves it out of this list into Been there, where you can set the date and add photos. Photos are resized and stored in this browser on this device — never uploaded, and they work offline. To carry one to another device, open it and choose Keep in the page; that saves a compressed copy into the page itself, visible anywhere you open this link. Fifty kept photos maximum, since the whole page has a size ceiling.
