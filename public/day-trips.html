Notes

The drive times assume a weekend. Every number is a door-to-door estimate from The Q Playa on a Saturday or Sunday with normal traffic, not a mileage calculation — and rounded up, because nothing in this city takes the time the map says. Treat them as planning numbers. Every entry has a Directions link that opens Maps with our address already set as the origin, which is where you get the real number for the hour you're actually leaving.

Menus. Each restaurant has a Menu link that goes straight to that exact restaurant's current menu, and a Map link for hours and the phone number. These resolve at the moment you tap them, so they keep working after a restaurant redesigns its site.

How these picks are chosen. Seafood leads wherever the geography supports it. No chicken anywhere. Beef only where the beef is the point — steakhouses and wood grills, not burger counters. Rooms are chosen for being pleasant to sit in; the handful of plain fish-market counters that made the cut are described as exactly that, because the shellfish justifies the picnic table. Where a town genuinely has nothing worth a detour, the note says so and points at the nearest place that does, rather than padding the list.

On Indian food. Southern California has no Indian restaurant at the level of Copra in San Francisco or Adda in New York. That is a real gap, not an oversight in the research. The two closest here are Mayura in Culver City, which is Keralan and genuinely excellent within that lane, and Bhanu's in Pasadena for South Indian dosas. Copra itself is listed on the San Francisco entry, since that is the trip where you can actually eat at it.

Michelin. Stars and Bib Gourmands come from the California guide and get reshuffled every year, so read them as worth checking rather than as fact. Book the starred rooms weeks ahead.

The plates. Each entry opens with an engraved plate, drawn by the page itself in one ink. Where this page is served from a normal web address it also fetches a photograph of the place from Wikipedia and lays it over the engraving, credited. The artifact viewer on claude.ai blocks outside images by policy, so there you get the engravings alone — same file, both behaviours, nothing broken either way.

Stamps and photos. Marking a place visited moves it out of this list into Been there, where you can set the date and add photos. Photos are resized and stored in this browser on this device — never uploaded, and they work offline. To carry one to another device, open it and choose Keep in the page; that saves a compressed copy into the page itself, visible anywhere you open this link. Fifty kept photos maximum, since the whole page has a size ceiling.
